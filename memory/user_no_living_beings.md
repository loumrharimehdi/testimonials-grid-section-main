**How to apply:**

- Even if a design (like a Frontend Mentor challenge) includes such images, offer alternatives: initials in a circle, abstract shapes, gradients, geometric patterns, or solid color placeholders
- Don't push back or ask them to reconsider — just propose alternatives naturally
